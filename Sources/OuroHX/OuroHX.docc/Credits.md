# Credits

Where each piece came from.

## Overview

Ouro HX for Apple was pulled out of two apps that shipped it first:

- [Spoonjoy](https://github.com/spoonjoy/spoonjoy-apple): motion and Reduce Motion, the haptic table, WCAG contrast,
  the tabletop layout and fold choice, and the Duo request-file protocol (in OuroHXTesting).
- [Gatefold](https://github.com/ourostack/gatefold): the four-way adaptive colour, readable labels, the book spread,
  the hinge observer and spine shadow.
- Both: `devicehub.swift`, byte-identical in each, is now `ouro-devicehub`.

The tokens are [ouro-ui-core](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui-core) v0.2.0's.
